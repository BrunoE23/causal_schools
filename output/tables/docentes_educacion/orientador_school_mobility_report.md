# Orientador movement across schools, 2018--2025

Universe: annual records satisfying `PERSONAS == 1` and `ID_IFP == 9` (main-position orientadores). A move is a change in `RBD`.

## Main findings

- The panel contains 17,399 orientador-year observations for 4,723 unique orientadores.
- Among 3,439 orientadores observed in at least two years, 351 (10.2%) are observed at more than one RBD during 2018--2025.
- Across 12,426 consecutive-year orientador transitions, 258 (2.1%) involve a change in RBD.

The ever-moved measure includes changes separated by gaps in observation. The annual rates below require the person to be observed in the selected function in both adjacent years.
