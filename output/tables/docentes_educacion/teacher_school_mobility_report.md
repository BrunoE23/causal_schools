# Teacher movement across schools, 2018--2025

Universe: annual records satisfying `PERSONAS == 1` and `ID_IFP == 1` (main-position classroom teachers). A move is a change in `RBD`.

## Main findings

- The panel contains 1,682,988 teacher-year observations for 315,092 unique teachers.
- Among 276,494 teachers observed in at least two years, 98,906 (35.8%) are observed at more than one RBD during 2018--2025.
- Across 1,326,432 consecutive-year teacher transitions, 121,978 (9.2%) involve a change in RBD.

The ever-moved measure includes changes separated by gaps in observation. The annual rates below require the teacher to be observed in both adjacent years.
