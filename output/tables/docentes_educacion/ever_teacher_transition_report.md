# Ever-classroom teacher careers and function transitions, 2018--2025

A person enters the cohort if they have `ID_IFP == 1` in at least one year. All of their main-position records (`PERSONAS == 1`) are then retained, regardless of function.

## Person-level persistence

- There are 315,092 ever-classroom teachers. Of these, 269,881 (85.7%) are observed only in the target function.
- Among 284,073 people observed in at least two years, 238,862 (84.1%) never change recorded main function.
- Conversely, 45,211 (14.3%) are observed in at least one other main function.

## What happens after a classroom teacher year

- Of 1,463,556 target-function transition-years, 1,359,200 (92.9%) are observed in any main position the next year.
- Among those observed, 1,326,432 (97.6%) remain in the target function and 32,768 (2.4%) change function.
- Among those observed, 132,432 (9.7%) change RBD across all next-year functions. Conditional on remaining in the target function, 121,978 (9.2%) change RBD.
