# Ever-orientador careers and function transitions, 2018--2025

A person is defined as an orientador if they have `ID_IFP == 9` in at least one year. Once identified, all of that person's main-position records (`PERSONAS == 1`) are retained, regardless of function.

## Person-level persistence

- There are 4,723 ever-orientadores. Of these, 1,587 (33.6%) are observed only as orientadores in every available year.
- Restricting to the 4,512 people observed in at least two years, 1,376 (30.5%) never change recorded main function.
- Conversely, 3,136 (66.4%) are observed in at least one non-orientador main function.

## What happens after an orientador year

This section starts from every `ID_IFP == 9` person-year in 2018--2024, then searches all main functions in the following year.

- Of 15,178 orientador transition-years, 13,952 (91.9%) are observed in any main position the next year; 1,226 are not observed.
- Among those observed next year, 12,426 (89.1%) remain orientadores and 1,526 (10.9%) change main function.
- Among those observed next year, 793 (5.7%) change RBD, whether or not they also change function.
- Conditional on remaining an orientador, 258 (2.1%) change RBD.
