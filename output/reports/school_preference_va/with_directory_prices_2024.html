All columns use the same complete submitted rankings in an exact Plackett-Luce model, conditional on the schools listed; unlisted alternatives are outside this exercise. VAs and all four school outcome averages are measured per one destination-school SD. The three new shares use equal weights across unique destination schools; existing VA and PAES normalizations are unchanged. Non-TP is the reference school type. Slopes are common across school types.

Observed outcome shares use grade-8 cohorts 2017-2020 (ages 12-16 at grade 8), grouped by the high school attended most. The exam-taking share covers all eligible students. Field and institution enrollment shares are among exam takers, counting non-enrollees as zero. PAES M1 is the current school math-score average, not a value-added measure.

Directory fees are a 2024 snapshot and PAES M1 is measured in the 2025 admission process, after the 2018-2020 applications. Coefficients describe conditional ranking associations, not causal price effects or information necessarily available at application. Changes across logit specifications can also reflect utility-scale changes.
