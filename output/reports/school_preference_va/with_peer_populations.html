Reading the results: Higher peer baseline SIMCE is positively associated with ranking a school earlier in all three versions. Peer composition adds information conditional on VA, and VA remains jointly predictive conditional on peers. In these models the VA block adds more incremental log likelihood than the peer block. This exercise does not support the stronger claim that peers dominate VA.

Incremental fit of peers versus VA

Each block has four coefficients. Peer gains compare D + peers with D. VA gains compare D + peers with the same model omitting only its four VAs; three auxiliary nested fits are saved for this purpose. These are in-sample ranking-fit comparisons, not causal willingness-to-pay measures or out-of-sample predictions.

Peer definitions, normalization and coverage

Use the supplied mean_equal_cohort columns: equally weight available school-cohort means in 2018-2020. Attendees have all three cohorts; applicant and offer groups sometimes have fewer. Retain source cohort-count and missing-data coverage fields. There is no additional imputation and no reconstruction of peer populations.

Applicant peers use simulated admission-probability weights. Offered peers include all recorded first-round offers, including guaranteed offers. Attendee peers include all master-universe students in grade-8 cohorts 2018-2020 whose most-attended HS is the destination, including non-applicants and without an age restriction. The strictly stochastic offer supplement is not used.

All peer columns share the same normalization: equally weighted attendee-school means and SDs over the 99 estimation destinations. A one-unit peer coefficient means Peer parents' education: 1.151 years; Peer baseline SIMCE: 0.368 student z-score units; Peer female share: 0.195 share units; Peer income decile: 1.127 deciles. Existing VA normalization is unchanged from D, so raw coefficient magnitudes are not a standalone comparison of peer and VA importance.

Peer parent education and income decile are strongly correlated across schools (correlations 0.84–0.92). Individual peer coefficients condition on the other three peer measures and can have unstable signs. Peer composition comes from the same 2018-2020 populations and is potentially endogenous; these are conditional ranking associations.
