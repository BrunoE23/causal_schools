Non-TP: The largest positive point estimate is Exam-taking VA at 0.1535 (SE 0.0043). Negative total point estimates occur for High-premium-institution VA. Total slopes not distinguished from zero at 5% are Math VA.

The four base VA coefficients in the full regression table apply only to non-TP schools. For mixed HC/TP and TP-only schools, add the matching interaction coefficient to obtain the total slope shown above. A positive or negative interaction describes a slope difference from non-TP schools, not the sign of that type's total slope. The distance coefficient is -0.1912 with published student coordinates and -0.0692 with home-comuna centers. In the main model greater distance is associated with lower relative ranking utility, conditional on the four VA measures, both school-type indicators and all eight interactions.

Using home-comuna centers leaves the signs of all twelve school-type-specific total VA slopes unchanged. The largest absolute change in a total VA slope is 0.0098.

Mixed HC/TP school has coefficient 0.3387 (SE 0.0053) with published coordinates and 0.3367 with home-comuna centers. At all four standardized VAs equal to zero, the main coefficient corresponds to 1.4031 times the conditional relative odds compared with omitted non-TP schools. For other VA values the type premium also includes all four type-by-VA interaction products. TP-only school has coefficient 0.3850 (SE 0.0094) with published coordinates and 0.3577 with home-comuna centers. At all four standardized VAs equal to zero, the main coefficient corresponds to 1.4696 times the conditional relative odds compared with omitted non-TP schools. For other VA values the type premium also includes all four type-by-VA interaction products.

These are conditional associations with submitted ordering. They do not establish that families observe or causally respond to VA; unmeasured school attributes may account for the associations. The model does not explain which schools enter a portfolio.

Each pooled 2017-2020 EB index is centered and divided by its sample SD (N-1 denominator) across unique destination schools in the final common sample. Each school has equal normalization weight, independently of application frequency or list length. Both models and every application year use the same indices and moments. No VA is re-estimated or imputed.

Geography sources, privacy displacement and limitations
  • Main distance uses every published student coordinate, including quality 4 municipality imputations.
  • municipality_imputed flags quality 4; no applicant is dropped or re-geocoded because of that quality code.
  • Robustness replaces only the student location by the center of the grade-8 residence comuna; the same actual A1 school-program campus remains the destination.
  • Distances are straight-line Haversine distances in kilometers, longitude first, using mean Earth radius 6371.0088 km. Regressors are log1p(distance_km).
  • Published student locations refer to the declared location and can differ from the current residence.
  • 2018 privacy noise: independent uniform perturbations of latitude and longitude between -0.0013 and +0.0013 degrees. Manual reports mean displacement 101 m and maximum 198 m.
  • 2019-2020 privacy noise: uniform direction 0-360 degrees and uniform radial displacement 50-300 m, mean 175 m. B1 draws are repeated when displaced points are not on land.
  • Quality 1 is rooftop/range_interpolated; quality 2 is geometric_center/approximate; quality 3 is the center of multiple coherent geocoder responses; quality 4 is municipality imputation; quality 5 is a user-shared location.
  • Comuna centers come from the existing comuna_info.RDS lookup, generated from raw/spatial/chl_admin_boundaries/chl_admin3.shp center_lat/center_lon (WGS84). They are comuna representative centers, not municipal-office coordinates.
  • Known source-label error: comuna_info.RDS and its source DBF call code 1401 TOCOPILLA. That code is Pozo Almonte; the distinct actual Tocopilla code is 2301. Joins use numeric codes, preserve each code's coordinates, and use the official residence name from universe_controls.csv. home_comuna_name_corrected flags code 1401; no raw or existing clean source file is altered.
  • The paper's 2018-2020 residence records already use the new 16xxx Nuble comuna codes; all match the lookup. No historical-code crosswalk is applied.
  • Some RBDs have multiple campus coordinates. Coordinates attach to rbd/cod_curso/process, so downstream school collapse keeps the first explicitly submitted program's campus, never an arbitrary school-level row.
  • Every submitted program has an A1 coordinate match in the audited paper sample. No school-directory fallback is used.
  • Coordinate validity checks use global latitude/longitude ranges and do not discard Easter Island or Antarctic records.

Likelihood and inference

The Plackett-Luce likelihood multiplies each ranked-school choice probability over schools remaining in that submitted list. The final singleton factor is one. A stratified Cox representation with unique event ranks evaluates this exact likelihood. Expanded conditional logit independently verifies coefficients, likelihood and student-cluster covariance on complete validation rankings. There is no intercept, school fixed effect, outside option or unlisted alternative. The four VA measures, two school-type indicators, all eight VA-by-type interactions and distance are the complete specification. The only sensitivity fit replaces student points with home-comuna centers.
