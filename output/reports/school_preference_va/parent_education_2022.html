Do families value school effectiveness?

Preference models with average parent education as a school attribute

Interpretation: Per one school-level SD in average parent education (1.42 years), the estimated utility association is Non-TP -0.0390 (SE 0.0046); Mixed HC/TP -0.1075 (SE 0.0072); TP only -0.0679 (SE 0.0117). These are total slopes: the baseline parent-education coefficient plus the relevant school-type interaction. The point estimates are negative for Non-TP, Mixed HC/TP, TP only. The specification includes all four EB VAs, PAES M1, parent education, distance, two school-type indicators, and each of the six attribute-by-type interactions.The parent-survey measure comes from 2nd-year high-school students in SIMCE 2022, after the 2018–2020 SAE applications. Its coefficient is descriptive and does not establish that families observed these later school averages when they applied.

For each student, I average the available mother and father education-year reports. I then average these family means across 2nd-year high-school SIMCE respondents at each RBD. One-school SD = 1.42 years, with each school receiving equal weight in normalization. Unknown, blank, and double-marked responses are missing; when only one parent response is valid, it contributes the family mean.

The two SIMCE items ask how far the student’s mother (or father’s partner) and father (or mother’s partner) advanced in education. Year conversions follow the response categories; tertiary categories are approximate years.
