Do families value school effectiveness?

Adding enrollment and monthly fee bands from the 2024 school directory

Same applicants before and after adding fees. The pooled model uses 273,184 applicants, 969,377 applicant-school pairs and 2,481 destination schools. The largest absolute change among the pooled VA slopes is 0.0069 utility units per school SD. The estimated paid-band utility coefficients range from -0.2223 to 0.1794 relative to a free school in the corresponding fee field. The largest movements in the four VA slopes are: TP only, High-premium-institution VA: -0.0165 before fees and -0.0096 with fees (change 0.0069); Mixed HC/TP, Math VA: -0.0536 before fees and -0.0474 with fees (change 0.0062); Non-TP, Exam-taking VA: 0.1110 before fees and 0.1081 with fees (change -0.0029).

Interpretation: These are conditional ranking associations within each submitted school list. The directory is a 2024 snapshot, after the 2018-2020 application decisions. Its fee bands describe later school prices; these estimates do not identify a causal price effect or the prices families faced when applying.

Price associations in the pooled model

Each paid-band row compares that fee band with free in the same field, holding the other fee field, all seven school attributes, type indicators, type interactions and distance constant. The same schools lack information for both fee fields, so one shared unknown-fee indicator captures their association and compares them with free in both fields. Unknown prices are never coded as free. Parentheses contain student-clustered standard errors.
