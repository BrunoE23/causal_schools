A total slope describes the change in ranking utility from a one-school-SD increase in that VA within the stated school type, holding other VA dimensions and distance fixed. The exponential of a total slope is the corresponding multiplier of the conditional odds of selecting that school next among those remaining in the submitted list. These are ordering associations, not causal responses to effectiveness or explanations of which schools enter a portfolio. Separate logits normalize unobserved utility errors separately: differences in coefficients across groups may reflect different error scales as well as different preferences. No formal cross-group equality tests are reported.

The mixed HC/TP and TP-only indicator coefficients in the full tables compare school types only when all four standardized VAs equal zero, the pooled reference means. With interactions, the conditional type difference changes with the school's VA vector.
