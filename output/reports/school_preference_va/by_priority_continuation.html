Conditional preference patterns

High-premium-institution VA has negative main point estimates in both school-ending groups (-0.0250 for non-prioritario and -0.0444 for prioritario) and positive estimates in both voluntary groups (0.0461 and 0.0425, respectively).

Math VA, Exam-taking VA, High-premium-field VA have positive point estimates in all four main fits.

School ends: non-prioritario: The largest positive VA point estimate is Exam-taking VA (0.1039, SE 0.0040), corresponding to 1.1095 times the conditional odds per pooled school SD. Negative conditional point estimates are High-premium-institution VA (-0.0250). The TP coefficient is 0.2749 (SE 0.0079).

School ends: prioritario: The largest positive VA point estimate is High-premium-field VA (0.0576, SE 0.0019), corresponding to 1.0593 times the conditional odds per pooled school SD. Negative conditional point estimates are High-premium-institution VA (-0.0444). The TP coefficient is 0.3555 (SE 0.0063).

Voluntary: non-prioritario: The largest positive VA point estimate is Exam-taking VA (0.0761, SE 0.0108), corresponding to 1.0791 times the conditional odds per pooled school SD. The TP coefficient is 0.2467 (SE 0.0212).

Voluntary: prioritario: The largest positive VA point estimate is Exam-taking VA (0.0736, SE 0.0107), corresponding to 1.0763 times the conditional odds per pooled school SD. The TP coefficient is 0.3035 (SE 0.0224).

These coefficients describe ordering associations among schools remaining in a submitted portfolio, holding the other five regressors fixed. They do not identify causal responses to effectiveness or explain which schools enter a portfolio. Separate logits normalize unobserved utility errors separately: differences in coefficients across groups may reflect different error scales as well as different preferences. No formal cross-group equality tests are reported.
