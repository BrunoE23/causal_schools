Conditional preference patterns

High-premium-institution VA has negative main point estimates in both school-ending groups (-0.0283 for non-prioritario and -0.0487 for prioritario) and positive estimates in both voluntary groups (0.0445 and 0.0339, respectively).

Math VA, Exam-taking VA, High-premium-field VA have positive point estimates in all four main fits.

School ends: non-prioritario: The largest positive VA point estimate is Exam-taking VA (0.1165, SE 0.0041), corresponding to 1.1236 times the conditional odds per pooled school SD. Negative conditional point estimates are High-premium-institution VA (-0.0283). Relative to the omitted non-TP category, Mixed HC/TP school has coefficient 0.2487 (SE 0.0081), or 1.2823 times the conditional odds; TP-only school has coefficient 0.3940 (SE 0.0114), or 1.4829 times the conditional odds.

School ends: prioritario: The largest positive VA point estimate is Exam-taking VA (0.0596, SE 0.0031), corresponding to 1.0615 times the conditional odds per pooled school SD. Negative conditional point estimates are High-premium-institution VA (-0.0487). Relative to the omitted non-TP category, Mixed HC/TP school has coefficient 0.3296 (SE 0.0065), or 1.3904 times the conditional odds; TP-only school has coefficient 0.4575 (SE 0.0085), or 1.5801 times the conditional odds.

Voluntary: non-prioritario: The largest positive VA point estimate is Exam-taking VA (0.0869, SE 0.0111), corresponding to 1.0908 times the conditional odds per pooled school SD. Relative to the omitted non-TP category, Mixed HC/TP school has coefficient 0.2252 (SE 0.0218), or 1.2526 times the conditional odds; TP-only school has coefficient 0.3559 (SE 0.0342), or 1.4275 times the conditional odds.

Voluntary: prioritario: The largest positive VA point estimate is Exam-taking VA (0.0964, SE 0.0109), corresponding to 1.1012 times the conditional odds per pooled school SD. Relative to the omitted non-TP category, Mixed HC/TP school has coefficient 0.2548 (SE 0.0230), or 1.2903 times the conditional odds; TP-only school has coefficient 0.5001 (SE 0.0310), or 1.6488 times the conditional odds.

These coefficients describe ordering associations among schools remaining in a submitted portfolio, holding the other six regressors fixed. They do not identify causal responses to effectiveness or explain which schools enter a portfolio. Separate logits normalize unobserved utility errors separately: differences in coefficients across groups may reflect different error scales as well as different preferences. No formal cross-group equality tests are reported.
