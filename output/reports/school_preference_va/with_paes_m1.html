Four VA dimensions plus school-average PAES M1, with separate slopes for non-TP, mixed HC/TP and TP-only schools. Each joint rank-ordered logit includes five metrics, two type indicators, ten interactions and distance.

PAES process 2025 measures the regular late-2024 M1 sitting among 2024 graduates. These scores postdate the 2018-2020 SAE rankings. The score mean is a retrospective school characteristic, not information observed by families when they applied. It covers M1 test takers rather than all students; MATE1_MAX and a 2024-process fallback are not used.
