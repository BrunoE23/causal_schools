Reading the results: Higher peer baseline SIMCE is positively associated with ranking a school earlier in all three versions. The four peer characteristics improve in-sample ranking fit more than the four VAs in all three versions. These comparisons describe conditional associations; they do not establish causal willingness to pay for peers or VA.

Adding attendee peers changes the math-VA coefficient from 0.049 to -0.018 and exam-taking VA from 0.079 to -0.002. Premium-field VA remains positive (0.054). The negative conditional income-decile coefficients do not imply that families dislike affluent peers: income, parent education and baseline achievement enter jointly and overlap strongly.

Peer definitions, normalization and coverage

The expanded measures reproduce all values of the original 100-school input. Available school-cohort means are equally weighted over 2018-2020; group-specific cohort counts and baseline-data coverage remain in the source tables. Missing school-group measures are not imputed.

Applicant peers use simulated admission-probability weights. Offered peers include all recorded first-round offers, including guaranteed offers. Attendee peers include all master-universe students in grade-8 cohorts 2018-2020 whose most-attended HS is the destination, including non-applicants and without an age restriction. The strictly stochastic offer supplement is not used.

All peer columns share the same normalization: equally weighted attendee-school means and SDs over the 2,454 estimation destinations. A one-unit peer coefficient means Peer parents' education: 1.469 years; Peer baseline SIMCE: 0.364 student z-score units; Peer female share: 0.150 share units; Peer income decile: 1.450 deciles. Existing VA normalization is unchanged from D, so raw coefficient magnitudes are not a standalone comparison of peer and VA importance.

Peer parent education and income decile are strongly correlated across schools (correlations 0.80–0.95). Individual peer coefficients condition on the other three peer measures and can have unstable signs. Peer composition comes from the same 2018-2020 populations and is potentially endogenous; these are conditional ranking associations.
