Do families value school effectiveness?

Preference models adding the existing grade-4-SIMCE income decile

Income-decile results: The total utility slope for a one-school-SD increase in mean baseline income decile is Non-TP -0.0796 (SE 0.0076); Mixed HC/TP 0.0176 (SE 0.0117); TP only -0.0262 (SE 0.0153). Each slope includes the school-type interaction and conditions on the other six school attributes, school type and distance. The coefficients are conditional associations, not causal effects of peers.

We reuse the existing mean school income decile from grade-4 SIMCE parent-survey data. It averages the established observed/imputed student income_decile_imputed measure among 2017–2020 grade-8 students attending each RBD. School means are standardized across destination schools with equal school weights. This is a later student-composition measure and was not necessarily known to families when they applied.
