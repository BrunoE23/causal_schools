Specification and interpretation

The full model contains M, V, M^2, V^2, MV, M^3, V^3, M^2V and MV^2, nine income-decile indicators (D5 reference), and admission-year indicators. Both full and reduced models also include reported home-region indicators. Scores retain their original within-admission-year standardization; income deciles retain the existing imputed baseline-income classification. The reduced model drops only the income indicators, retaining the identical students and all other controls.

Income share = (full R-squared - R-squared without income) / full R-squared. This is income's incremental contribution when added after scores, admission year, and home region, as a fraction of the variation explained by the full model. It does not allocate explanatory power shared by income and the other controls. It is not a causal share. All R-squared values are ordinary unadjusted, in-sample measures.

Income's partial R-squared instead divides the R-squared gain by 1 minus reduced-model R-squared. It describes the fraction of previously unexplained variation removed by adding income, and is saved separately in income_contribution.csv. It is not the percentage displayed as income share.

Income rows below are OLS coefficients relative to decile 5. Score rows are average partial derivatives of the full cubic polynomial per SD, not finite one-SD changes. Coefficients and derivatives are multiplied by 100, with actual attended-school-clustered CR1 standard errors in parentheses. Inference uses t(G-1); the nine income coefficients are tested jointly using a clustered Wald F test.

The common sample has 93,423 students in 2,735 schools, including 8,522 HE non-enrollees. The sample requires original z_math >= 1 and exactly matches the preceding high-math HE-enrollment regression. Scores are not restandardized and income deciles are not re-ranked. There is no HE-enrollment or school-VA-availability condition. The 840 admission-2026 records have zero recorded premium outcomes and remain in OLS; this is not evidence about future enrollment.

Main outcomes are enrollment in a premium institution, a premium field, either (inclusive union), and both. All outcomes are zero for non-enrollees. R-squared contributions and standard errors do not add across these outcomes.

Checks verify common samples, normal equations, full and reduced clustered covariance matrices, the nested-model projection identity, and the coefficient identity either = institution + field - both in both specifications. LPM fitted values are not clipped; fractions outside [0,1] are retained in the analysis diagnostics.

Region of origin is operationalized as CODIGO_REGION_DOMICILIO: reported home region in the PAES/PDT socioeconomic-domicile file for the student's existing admission year. Match by MRUN and ANYO_PROCESO = psu_year. All 93,423 students match with a valid code 1-16, so no region imputation or sample exclusion is needed. This is admissions-period residence, not birthplace or necessarily grade-8 residence. Region code 1 is omitted as a nuisance reference; baseline family-income deciles are unchanged.

Independent D1-reference fits verify that changing the income reference to D5 leaves fitted values and R-squared unchanged and gives the correct transformed coefficient covariance. Separate diagnostics compare the models with and without region controls on exactly the same sample.
