HE enrollment on parent education only

OLS: HE enrollment = intercept + father schooling years + mother schooling years. Both parents enter jointly and continuously. No other controls or high-math restriction.

Start with all 931,547 broad-panel students (grade-8 cohorts 2017-2020, ages 12-16, attended school observed). Retain 775,268 with both parent measures available; exclude 156,279 with remaining missing education. No restrictions on exam-taking, scores, income, region, or estimated school VA.

Education uses the existing grade-4 SIMCE category-to-approximate-years conversion and existing baseline-context imputations: 103,243 father and 74,011 mother values are imputed; 663,163 students report both. No new imputation is performed. Each coefficient conditions on the other parent's schooling. These are associations, not causal effects.

SEs cluster by attended high school with CR1 finite-sample correction and t(G-1) intervals. Enrollment and admission-year records are kept as recorded in the existing data. The model includes no admission-year effects.
