The population is timely grade-9 SAE applicants in 2018-2020 whose grade-8 origin is classified as either ending at grade 8 or offering grade 9. School ending is the operational non-voluntary group; voluntary means the origin offers grade 9 and does not establish the student's motive or an eventual school change. Prioritario is the applicant's SAE low-income priority indicator. Expected VA is the admission-probability-weighted value added of the application portfolio. Attended VA uses the high school attended most often and the paper's unchanged EB school VA. Math and verbal VA are in student-outcome SD units; binary-outcome VA is in percentage points. Means use available observations, and the underlying CSV reports coverage for every cell.
