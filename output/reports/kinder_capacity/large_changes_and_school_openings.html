Interpretation

  • First observed operating means ESTADO_ESTAB=1 and MATRICULA=1 for the first time in the available annual directory history. These are candidate openings, not confirmed legal founding dates. A school previously operating before the 2015 baseline, or receiving a new RBD, could be misclassified.
  • Previously registered schools may have been authorized but awaiting enrollment or inactive. We do not call these confirmed reactivations. The 2015 directory is the baseline; no opening counts are shown for that left-censored year.
  • Preschool means an ENS teaching code of 10 in the first operating year; this combines pre-kinder and kinder. Public/subsidized means COD_DEPE other than 4; private paid means COD_DEPE=4. The directory series covers its registered RBD universe, not every JUNJI/Integra provider.
  • SAE cupos_totales measure declared total capacity. Vacantes measure places available to SAE applicants after continuity. The CSV also reports school counts with vacancy changes greater than 10, but these do not measure capacity expansion.
  • Directory opening counts are broader than SAE participation, refer to actual directory year, and must not be aligned mechanically with SAE entry years. Directory-only data cannot tell us the kinder seat capacity of all candidate openings.
  • School entry/exit and its capacity remain in the full capacity accounting. No causal effect of the compulsory-kinder proposal is identified by these series.
