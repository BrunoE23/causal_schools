Each bar is the share of all schools with VA in that region whose VA falls in the bin. Its colored segments show the lottery categories. All bars together sum to 100% within each region; a color's total share equals its fraction of schools in that region. Each school has equal weight.

There are 30 equal-width bins spanning the full observed VA range, with common bin boundaries and axis scales across regions within each measure. All group sizes are included. Math uses test-score SD; field and institution use percentage points. These histograms use the whole region as the denominator; the previous density curves normalized separately within each lottery group.

The 2017-2020 All-sample EB-adjusted observational VA values and their original centering are unchanged. The same 25 classified schools lack VA. Lottery variation means at least one simulated grade-9 applicant with 0 < p < 1 in a school-year, after summing programs within school (1e-10 endpoint tolerance). Always/sometimes/never refers to observed SAE offering years in 2018-2020; Metropolitana enters in 2019. Classification details.
