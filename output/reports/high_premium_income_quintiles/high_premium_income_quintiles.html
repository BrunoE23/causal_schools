For each model, predict every student under each quintile while holding their observed scores and admission year fixed, then average the probability difference from Q1. Parentheses are delta-method HC1 robust SEs. These are discrete quintile contrasts, not log-odds coefficients.

Quintiles combine adjacent existing imputed baseline SIMCE parental-income deciles. Outcome flags are the unchanged master variables high_paying_field_m1 and high_inst_m1 at first observed HE enrollment. Non-enrollees retain zero in the all-student models; no monetary income imputation is used for these binary outcomes. Both scores, baseline income quintile and both outcomes must be observed.

Admission-year cells with no variation in either outcome are omitted from both outcome models within each sample, because fixed-effect logits supply no finite identifying information there. Exclusions: all_students: 5105 students (years 2026); enrolled_only: 0 students (years ).
