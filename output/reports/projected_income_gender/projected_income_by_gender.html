The original broad panel covers grade-8 cohorts 2017–2020, ages 12–16 with a valid attended high school. The unchanged income sample has 931,546 students with known gender, including 625,616 HE enrollees. Adding scores requires both positive observed entrance-exam components and an observed admission year; missing tests are not assigned zero scores. Scores are standardized within admission year before regression.

Income is the existing master outcome at first observed HE enrollment. Non-enrollees retain CLP 553,553; enrollees retain the same hierarchical MiFuturo projections. These are conditional program-income associations, rather than observed individual wages or causal gender effects.
