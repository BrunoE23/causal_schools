All models condition on original standardized entrance-exam math at least one SD above its admission-year mean. Controls: the full bivariate cubic in math and verbal (nine terms), income-decile indicators (D5 omitted), admission-year indicators, and PAES-reported domicile-region indicators.

Parent education comes from the grade-4 SIMCE parent questionnaire. Mother and father are separate variables. The existing category-to-years coding is retained: completed university = 16, master = 18, doctorate = 21; vocational and incomplete tertiary categories are approximate. The main model uses reported values plus existing baseline-context median imputations; the second column requires both reported. Remaining missing values are excluded, with coverage recorded separately.

The third column additionally excludes admission 2026, whose enrollment outcomes are all zero in this data vintage. The first two columns preserve those recorded zeros, as in the previous OLS analysis. Inference uses attended-school CR1 covariance, including all model parameters in its finite-sample correction, and t(G-1) confidence intervals. These are conditional associations, not identified causal effects. No new imputation, score normalization, or income ranking is performed.
