# Applicant comparison by continuation and priority

For the four-way descriptive comparison requested on 2026-10-14, the applicant
population is the paper's timely grade-9 SAE applicants in cohorts 2018-2020 whose
grade-8 origin is classified in the contemporaneous SAE offering file.

“School ending” means the origin offers no grade above grade 8 and is the operational
non-voluntary group. “Voluntary” means the origin offers grade 9. This classification
does not observe motive and does not require that the applicant eventually changes
schools. Applicants with unresolved or other origin-offering patterns are excluded.
These two groups are crossed with the applicant-level SAE `prioritario` indicator.

Application portfolios are summarized by the same probability-weighted expected EB
VA used in the main paper. Attended-school VA is the unchanged EB VA of the student's
`most_time_RBD`. Math and verbal VA remain in student-outcome SD units; VA for binary
outcomes is multiplied by 100 and shown in percentage points. TP and private-paid
school flags reuse the definitions in the existing Table 2 and TP follow-up scripts.
