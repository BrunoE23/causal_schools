# Decision: Report first-round SAE offer compliance

**Date:** 2026-09-22
**Status:** active

## Decision

Measure offer compliance as whether the student's most-time high school after
grade 8 equals the school in the student's positive first-round SAE offer.
Report three distinct quantities so the denominator is explicit:

- the fraction receiving a positive first-round offer;
- the fraction of the full lottery-risk sample attending the offered school,
  treating students without an offer as non-takers;
- the fraction attending the offered school conditional on receiving an offer.

Report these quantities overall, by SAE cohort, gender, grade-4 math quintile,
baseline income quintile, and within each main-table outcome sample. Produce
separate tables for the maximum-data pairing (VA 2017--2020, SAE 2018--2020)
and the no-overlap pairing (VA 2017--2018, SAE 2019--2020).

This is a descriptive take-up measure. The continuous first-stage coefficient
and F statistic in the main IV table remain the relevant diagnostics for the
school-value instrument itself.

As a complementary diagnostic, compare the student's eventual most-time high
school with positions 1--3 in the submitted SAE application list, separately
for students with and without a recorded first-round offer. Rank-specific
shares are conditional on having submitted a school at that rank. Also report
attendance at any of the top three choices and the offer-minus-no-offer
difference.
